RaksHex

Deciding when an agent may act.

An evolving control-plane project for consequential AI-agent actions.

View source ↗Open project site ↗
PROJECT CONTEXTPrivate beta · pre-incorporation · Founder-led, part-time engineering team

THE PREMISE

What I wanted to solve

A valid API credential alone is not proof that a particular automated action should be authorized. Teams also need a record of what was requested and why a decision was made.

THE BUILD

How it works

The RaksHex project is exploring delegated authority, policy checks, credential mediation and auditable records for actions taken by AI systems. It is a private-beta project, not an incorporated company or a product with paying customers.
